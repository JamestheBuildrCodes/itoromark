Common Questions

Before You Apply

Is this really 1-on-1 with Itoro himself?
Yes. Every session is directly with Itoro Mark — not a team member, not a student assistant. That's why spots are strictly limited per cohort.
What if I'm a complete beginner?
The Foundation programme is suitable for beginners. However, Itoro recommends completing the free CTC Academy fundamentals first so your sessions can go deeper faster. Apply and he'll tell you honestly which programme fits you.
How are sessions held?
Sessions are held via Google Meet or Zoom — video call with screen sharing. You can share your charts, your broker platform, even your trade journal. Itoro can see exactly what you're looking at.
Is there a refund policy?
Payment secures your spot and confirms Itoro's time commitment. Due to the limited availability of private spots, payments are non-refundable once a session schedule has been confirmed. If you have concerns before paying, WhatsApp Itoro first.
Can I pay in instalments?
Growth and Elite programmes have instalment options available on request. Mention this in your application and Itoro will discuss it with you directly.
What happens after I pay?
Itoro receives your application and payment confirmation immediately. He personally reviews your background and goals, then reaches out on WhatsApp within 24–48 hours to schedule your first session and send you a pre-session questionnaire.
